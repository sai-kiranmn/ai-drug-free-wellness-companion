AI for a Drug-Free Future

This project uses AI as an early support layer for emotional stress, peer pressure, and unhealthy coping risks.

Problem

Many young people face stress, loneliness, peer pressure, and emotional confusion. When support is not available early, some may move toward harmful habits.

Solution

Our AI wellness companion allows users to type or speak their feelings, receive calm AI guidance, and check their emotional risk level.

USP

The project combines voice-based AI conversation, mood check-ins, risk-level awareness, and drug-free prevention guidance in one simple website.

AI/ML Use

The system analyzes user text for stress signals, substance-risk keywords, emotional pressure, and then gives safe wellness-focused responses.
